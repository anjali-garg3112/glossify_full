Car detailing is a careful cleaning and finishing process for a vehicle’s exterior and interior. It goes beyond a quick wash by focusing on surfaces, finish and cabin cleanliness, helping your car look well cared for.

It depends on how often you drive, where you park and your car’s exposure to dust, sun and weather. Many owners consider a full detail every few months, with regular washes in between. We can help you choose a schedule for your car.

Car detailing means thoroughly cleaning and caring for a vehicle’s inside and outside. Depending on the service, it may include deep cleaning, polishing, paint enhancement and protective treatments.

A car wash mainly removes everyday dirt from the vehicle. Detailing is more thorough and can include careful interior cleaning, paint polishing and protective treatments, depending on the package you choose.

Some light surface marks and swirl marks may be reduced with suitable paint correction. Deep scratches that reach the paint or primer may need a repair instead. The result depends on the scratch, so an inspection is best.

Ceramic coating is a liquid protective treatment applied to suitable exterior surfaces. It can improve gloss and make routine cleaning easier. Preparation, maintenance and durability depend on the product and how the vehicle is used.
